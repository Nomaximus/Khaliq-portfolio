01 / The beginning

Curiosity became a method.

I became interested in Information Systems because every organization has a hidden architecture: the handoffs, rules, records, and decisions that quietly determine whether work moves forward.

My work starts by making that architecture visible. I map the process, question the friction, and shape a system that people can actually use when the pressure is real.

02 / The practice

Making it all work together.

The best system is not the one with the most features. It is the one that creates clarity for the people responsible for using it.

Enterprise systems

SAP S/4HANA, Odoo ERP modules, and BPMN workflow automation become useful when they connect operations instead of adding another isolated tool.

Data architecture

I design relational structures, ETL paths, and analytics layers that protect the meaning inside the data while making it ready for action.

Governance and risk

ITIL v4 and ISO 27001 principles turn reliability into an operating habit: clear ownership, measurable controls, and decisions that can be explained.
